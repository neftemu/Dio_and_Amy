1. Who we are

Purrfect Playtime (“the game”, “the app”) is a relaxing game in which you play with a cat using different toys. It is published on Google Play by Dio & Amelia (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. If you have questions, write to us at 0x646563@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

None. The game does not ask you to create an account or to enter your name, e-mail address, age, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information. We do not run a server that receives data from the app.

  • No advertising and no advertising ID is used.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are built into the app.
  • No crash-reporting service is built into the app.
  • No sign-in of any kind, including Google Play Games sign-in. There are no leaderboards, chat or other social features.

The sections below describe the few places where your device talks to Google services, so that you can see exactly what happens.

3. Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file on your device with: your coins, the cats and accessories you have unlocked, which accessory each cat wears, the number of refills, simple play counters (for example how many things you have broken), your sound setting, your language, and which video comes next. This file is not sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

The game picks its language from your phone’s language setting; this is read on the device only.

Android’s standard Auto Backup feature may copy this file to your own Google Drive backup, and restore it when you set up a new phone. That is controlled by your device and Google account settings (Settings → Google → Backup), not by us, and we cannot see it.

5. In-app purchases

The game is free. Some cat accessories (such as hats and bows) can be bought with real money as one-time purchases. Purchases are processed only by Google Play. We never see or receive your card or other payment details.

  • When you open the game, it asks Google Play which accessories your Google account already owns, so that purchases are restored when you reinstall the game or change phones. Google Play answers the app directly on your device.
  • As the developers, we may see information about orders in our Google Play Console (such as order number, product, country and amount) as provided by Google under its own terms. We do not use this to identify or contact players.
  • Refunds and purchase history are handled by Google Play. See Google Play refunds.

6. Videos (YouTube)

After you tap “Next room” the game may play a short, cat-themed video. The video is streamed from YouTube using YouTube’s own embedded player. Nothing is downloaded or saved by the game.

  • While a video plays, YouTube / Google can receive technical information from your device, such as your IP address, device and browser type, and cookies or similar identifiers, in line with Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information and we do not control how Google uses it.
  • The videos are made by other people. We do not control their content, and a video can be removed or changed by its owner at any time.
  • You can tap SKIP at any time (or press the Back button). If your device is offline, or a video cannot start, no video is shown and the game simply goes to the next room.
  • You can turn videos off. The game has a “Videos between rooms” button (a small TV icon with a play triangle). It is in the main menu, top left next to the sound button, and also in the top bar while you play in a room. Tap it to switch the videos on or off; a message confirms the new setting. Videos are on by default. When they are off, no video is requested, nothing is streamed from YouTube, and “Next room” goes straight to the next room. Your choice is remembered on your device until you change it.
  • You can also stop all streaming by turning off the game’s internet access in your Android settings (or by playing offline). The game works fully without internet, except for buying accessories.

8. Children’s privacy and a note for parents

Purrfect Playtime is made for everyone, not only for children. Because it has friendly cartoon cats and a family-home setting, it may also appeal to children. For that reason we follow the rules that protect children’s privacy, including the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR rules for children, and Google Play’s Families Policy.

What this means for privacy

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or under the applicable age in your country). The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no chat, no user-generated content and no links that take a child to other apps or websites.
  • Because we collect nothing, there is nothing for a parent to review, correct or delete on our side. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by e-mailing us), write to 0x646563@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.
  • Purchases: accessories can only be bought through Google Play, which asks for confirmation. Parents can require approval for, or block, in-app purchases with Google Play parental controls or Google Family Link.
  • Videos: the “Next room” videos are streamed from YouTube (see section 6). Parents who prefer that no video be streamed can tap the “Videos between rooms” button (top left of the main menu) to switch videos off, or switch off the game’s internet access. The game then goes straight to the next room.

Content note for parents

In this game the cat knocks over and breaks virtual household objects – vases, mugs, plates, lamps, picture frames and similar things – and the player earns coins for it. Children playing the game can make the cat break digital things that look like objects at home.

If you find this disturbing, or you think it could encourage your child to break real things at home, please do not let your child play this game.

The game itself includes a warning for this reason: in the main lobby a lucky-cat character waves, meows and shows a message:

“WARNING! Do NOT break things at home! Do it here, in the game. Breaking digital things won’t upset anyone!”

We encourage parents to talk with children about the difference between a game and real life. There is no blood, no people and no real violence in the game; the cat is never harmed.

9. Retention and deletion

Your progress file lives on your device only. To delete it, clear the app’s storage (Settings → Apps → Purrfect Playtime → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the game. Deleting the file does not delete purchases: those belong to your Google account and will be restored from Google Play. A Google backup of the file, if you use Auto Backup, can be managed in your Google account settings.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK, or California), you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction, deletion and objection. Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. Google acts independently for Google Play and YouTube, and you can use its tools at myaccount.google.com. You may always contact us at 0x646563@gmail.com with any privacy question or request.

11. Security

Since the game does not send your personal data to us, there is no database of player information to protect. Purchases and video streaming use secure (HTTPS) connections provided by Google. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we keep the app simple and minimal on purpose.

12. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if we add ads, analytics or accounts in the future), we will update this page and the “Effective date” at the top before the change is released, and we will update the information we give to Google Play. Please check this page from time to time.
